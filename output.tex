Domain & Dataset & Description \\ \hline
Speech Emotion Recognition & RAVDESS & The RAVDESS audio dataset contains 7,356 files from 24 actors (12 male, 12 female) speaking two statements in a neutral North American accent. It includes speech expressing 8 emotions (calm, happy, sad, angry, fearful, surprise, disgust, neutral) and song expressing 5 emotions (calm, happy, sad, angry, fearful). All audio files are 16-bit, 48kHz .wav format. \\ \hline
& TESS & The Toronto Emotional Speech Set (TESS) contains 2,800 audio recordings spoken by two female actors aged 26 and 64. The recordings include 200 target words spoken with seven emotions: anger, disgust, fear, happiness, pleasant surprise, sadness, and neutral. All files are high-quality WAV audio recordings and are widely used for speech emotion recognition tasks. \\ \hline
& EmoTa & The EmoTa dataset is an open-access Tamil Speech Emotion Recognition dataset containing 936 utterances from 22 native Tamil speakers. It includes five emotions: anger, happiness, sadness, fear, and neutral. The dataset is used for emotion classification tasks and supports research in Tamil speech emotion recognition. \\ \hline
Text Emotion Recognition & Tweet Emotions Dataset & The Tweet Emotions Dataset is a collection of english tweets labeled with different emotional categories such as joy, sadness, anger, love, fear, and surprise. It is commonly used in natural language processing (NLP) tasks to train and evaluate models for emotion detection in social media text. \\ \hline
& Sinhala-TER dataset & The Sinhala Emotion Dataset (Created) is a dataset developed by translating the Tweet Emotions Dataset into the Sinhala language. The original English tweets were converted into Sinhala while preserving their labeled emotions such as joy, sadness, anger, love, fear, and surprise. \\ \hline
& Rasa Tamil Emotions Dataset & The Rasa Tamil Emotions Dataset is a Tamil speech emotion dataset that contains audio recordings representing different human emotions. The dataset includes speech samples labeled with emotions such as happiness, sadness, anger, fear, and neutral, based on the Rasa theory of emotions from Indian aesthetics. \\ \hline
Large Language Model & English-llm Dataset & The English dataset includes synthetic text data labeled with emotions and paired with empathetic responses. It contains diverse conversational scenarios to train the model for recognizing user emotions and generating contextually appropriate responses. \\ \hline
& Sinhala-llm Dataset & The Sinhala dataset consists of synthetic text data labeled with emotions and matched with empathetic responses. It covers a range of everyday conversational situations, enabling the model to understand and respond effectively in Sinhala. \\ \hline
& Tamil-llm Dataset & The Tamil dataset comprises synthetic text data with labeled emotions and corresponding empathetic responses. It provides varied dialogue examples to train the model for accurate emotion recognition and response generation in Tamil. \\ \hline
Text to Speech & Karen (US Female) TTS Dataset & This public domain dataset features approximately 12 hours of clean, American-accented speech recorded by Karen Savage from three classic audiobooks. It contains 6,847 utterances (3–15 seconds each) at a 22,050 Hz sample rate, specifically processed using Whisper AI and Audacity for Piper TTS training. \\ \hline
& Multi speaker TTS dataset – Sinhala & This public domain dataset features approximately 12 hours of clean, American-accented English audio recorded by Karen Savage, specifically curated and formatted for training Piper TTS voices. It contains 6,847 high-quality utterances (3–15 seconds each) sourced from classic LibriVox audiobooks, ensuring a license-free, commercial-ready base for speech synthesis and fine-tuning. The audio was processed at 22,050 Hz using Audacity and SoX, with transcriptions automatically generated and cleaned via Whisper AI. \\ \hline
& Open SLR- tamil dataset & The SLR65 Tamil Speech Dataset is a crowdsourced dataset containing high-quality audio recordings of Tamil sentences spoken by native male and female speakers. It includes WAV audio files and TSV transcription files that link each recording to its corresponding text. \\ \hline
